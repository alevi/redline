# A Proposal for the Most Unreasonable Birthday Party Ever Conceived

## Executive Summary

This document proposes a birthday party of such singular ambition that future generations will speak of it in hushed, reverent tones. The honoree will be celebrated not merely with cake and candles, but with a full theatrical production, a live falcon, a trapeze, and at least one smoke machine.

## Theme: The Lost Civilization of Cake

The party will adopt the aesthetic of an undiscovered ancient civilization whose entire culture revolved around the baking and consumption of cake. Guests will arrive to find the venue transformed into an archaeological dig site, complete with half-buried fondant artifacts, hieroglyphics depicting the sacred frosting rituals, and a "chief archaeologist" (a hired actor) who will insist on carbon-dating the birthday cake before it is cut.

All decorations will be hand-fabricated from sugar paste, marzipan, and the tears of sous chefs.

## Venue

The party will be held in a rented aircraft hangar on the outskirts of town, chosen for its cathedral-like acoustics and its ability to accommodate a full-scale replica of a Mesopotamian ziggurat made entirely from Rice Krispie treats.

A secondary venue — a fog-filled antechamber — will serve as the "temporal transition zone" guests must pass through before entering the main hall. The fog will smell faintly of vanilla.

## The Guest Experience

### Arrival

Upon arrival, each guest will be handed:

- A parchment scroll with their "ancient name" (pre-assigned). Guests who wish to petition for a different ancient name may submit a written appeal to the Bureau of Appellate Nomenclature no later than 72 hours before the event. Appeals must include: (1) a compelling mythological justification, (2) a notarized statement of dissatisfaction with the assigned name, and (3) a small offering. The Bureau reserves the right to deny all petitions without explanation. The Bureau's decision is final. The Bureau is one person.
- A Munsell color chart (ceremonial)
- A name badge written in a custom cuneiform font commissioned specifically for this event

### The Falcon

A trained falcon named Gerald will be present throughout the event. Gerald will carry small scrolls between guests containing personalized birthday messages from the honoree. Gerald's handler, who goes exclusively by "Thornton," will be available for questions but has been briefed to answer only in riddles.

### Dinner

*Trimalchio would have approved.* Dinner will consist of eleven courses, each representing a different era of the fictional civilization's culinary history. The meal begins with the "Pre-Frosting Period" (a very plain cracker), passes through the "Golden Age of Buttercream" (an elaborate dessert involving a spun-sugar dome that is shattered tableside with a ceremonial mallet), and concludes with the "Twilight of Reasonable Restraint" — an era the civilization ultimately rejected, represented at table by a single unadorned grape served on a bare plate as a cautionary tale.

Wine pairings will be provided by a sommelier who will describe each selection using only geological metaphors.

### The Entertainment

A 13-piece chamber orchestra, disguised as archaeologists, will reveal themselves mid-party and perform an original suite titled *The Ballad of the Eternal Ganache*. This will be immediately followed by a 20-minute interpretive dance performed by the wait staff, who have been rehearsing since February.

The evening will conclude with a coordinated fireworks display synchronized to a recording of the birthday honoree saying "oh, you really shouldn't have" on a loop.

## The Cake

The cake requires its own section.

The cake will be eight tiers, each a different flavor, each frosted to represent a layer of geological strata. A small fondant dinosaur will be embedded in the third tier as a "fossil," discoverable only upon cutting. The top tier will be filled with confetti that deploys on cutting, because subtlety is the enemy of celebration.

The candles will be replaced with small controlled flares that burn in the colors of the honoree's choosing. A fire marshal will be on-site. His name is also Gerald.

## Budget

The total proposed budget is left deliberately vague, as the committee has found that specificity in matters of this kind tends to invite unnecessary interference from the reasonable members of the planning group.

A conservative estimate suggests the figure rhymes with "fifty thousand dollars."

## Why This Party

Because the honoree deserves far more fuss — but this is the fuss we could pull off. Because life is short and the window for renting an aircraft hangar and hiring a falcon is narrower than one might think. Because in twenty years, nobody will remember the sensible party with the nice venue and the tasteful flower arrangements, but everyone will remember Gerald.

Everyone always remembers Gerald.
